# FEAT-17: Apple Watch app + complications

**Priority:** P2
**Category:** Feature
**Effort:** High (1-2 weeks)
**Platform:** iOS (watchOS)
**Status:** Open
**Sprint:** [sprint-surfaces](../sprints/sprint-surfaces.md)

---

## Goal
A watchOS app where the user sees upcoming alarms and marks them done, plus watch-face complications for the next alarm. The phone keeps the alarm data and does all scheduling; alarms still fire from the phone (FB-01, AE-02).

## Notes
Council required: needs a new target, a WatchConnectivity sync design, a decision on whether completions made on the watch go back through the phone's completion path, and tier placement. The user chose a full app (2026-11-03).
