# FEAT-14: Checklist per alarm

**Priority:** P2
**Category:** Feature
**Effort:** Medium
**Platform:** iOS, Android
**Status:** Open
**Sprint:** [sprint-surfaces](../sprints/sprint-surfaces.md)

---

## Goal
A user can attach a short checklist to an alarm (e.g. "bring: insurance card, old plates"). It appears on the firing screen and the detail screen, and resets each cycle.

## Notes
Council required: needs a new model or relationship, decisions on per-cycle reset semantics, and sync. Tier decided by council.
