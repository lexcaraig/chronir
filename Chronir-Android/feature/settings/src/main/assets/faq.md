# Chronir FAQ

## General

**What is Chronir?**
An alarm app for things that come around every week, month or year: bills, refills, renewals, maintenance. When one is due, Chronir rings like a wake-up alarm, full screen, until you deal with it.

**Is Chronir free?**
Yes. The free version includes up to 3 alarms and works fully on your phone, with no account. Chronir Plus removes the limit and adds the extras listed below.

---

## Alarms & Scheduling

**What schedules can I set?**
- One-time, on a specific date
- Weekly, on the days you pick
- Monthly, on a date (the 15th) or a weekday (the last Friday)
- Yearly, on a date
- Every N days

**What if I pick the 31st and the month is shorter?**
The alarm rings on the last day of that month instead.

**Will my alarm ring when my phone is on silent or Do Not Disturb?**
Yes, unless you've changed Android's defaults. Chronir alarms are Android system alarms, and Do Not Disturb lets alarms through unless you've changed that setting.

**Do I need to keep the app open?**
No. Alarms are scheduled with Android and ring even if Chronir is closed. After a restart, Chronir sets them up again on its own.

**How does snooze work?**
When an alarm rings you can snooze it for 1 hour, 1 day or 1 week. With Plus you can also pick your own snooze length.

**Can I get a heads-up before an alarm?**
Yes. Pre-alarm warnings send a notification before the alarm is due, from 15 minutes up to 1 week ahead.

## Chronir Plus

**What does Plus include?**
- Unlimited alarms
- Custom snooze lengths
- Pre-alarm warnings up to a week ahead
- Completion history & streaks

**How much is it?**
The upgrade screen shows the price in your currency.

**How do I subscribe?**
Settings → Subscription → Upgrade to Plus. Payment goes through Google Play.

**Can I cancel anytime?**
Yes. Cancel in the Play Store → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. You keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for.

**What happens to my alarms if I cancel?**
They stay on your phone.

## Troubleshooting

**My alarm didn't ring.**
The most common cause is battery optimization. Some phone makers (Samsung, Xiaomi, OnePlus, Oppo, Vivo, Huawei) stop apps in the background to save power. In Chronir's Settings, under Alarm Reliability, check that these are all allowed:
- Battery optimization
- Alarms & reminders
- Full-screen alarms

**I'm not getting pre-alarm warnings.**
Warnings are notifications. In Chronir's Settings, under Alarm Reliability, check that Notifications are allowed.
