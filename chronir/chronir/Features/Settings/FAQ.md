# Chronir FAQ

## General

**What is Chronir?**
An alarm app for things that come around every week, month or year: bills, refills, renewals, maintenance. When one is due, Chronir rings like a wake-up alarm, full screen, until you deal with it.

**Is Chronir free?**
Yes. The free version includes up to 3 alarms and works fully on your iPhone, with no account. Chronir Plus removes the limit and adds the extras listed below.

---

## Alarms & Scheduling

**What schedules can I set?**
- One-time, on a specific date
- Weekly, on the days you pick
- Monthly, on a date (the 15th) or a weekday (the last Friday)
- Yearly, on a date
- Every N days

**What if I pick the 31st and the month is shorter?**
The alarm rings on the last day of that month instead.

**Will my alarm ring when my iPhone is on silent or in a Focus?**
Yes. Chronir uses iOS system alarms, which ring through silent mode and Focus, the same way the Clock app does.

**Do I need to keep the app open?**
No. Alarms are scheduled with iOS and ring even if Chronir is closed or your iPhone has restarted.

**How does snooze work?**
When an alarm rings you can snooze it for 1 hour, 1 day or 1 week. With Plus you can also pick your own snooze length.

**What is Slide to Stop?**
An optional setting (Settings → Slide to Stop) that makes you slide, rather than tap, to stop a ringing alarm, so it can't be silenced by accident.

**Can I get a heads-up before an alarm?**
Yes. Pre-alarm warnings send a notification before the alarm is due. Free includes 1 day before; Plus adds 1 hour, 3 days and 7 days.

## Chronir Plus

**What does Plus include?**
- Unlimited alarms
- More pre-alarm warnings
- Cloud backup & sync
- Completion history & streaks
- Custom snooze lengths
- Photo attachments
- Notes on alarms
- More alarm sounds

**How much is it?**
$1.99 a month or $19.99 a year, in US dollars. The upgrade screen shows the price in your currency.

**How do I subscribe?**
Settings → Subscription → Upgrade to Plus. Payment goes through your Apple Account.

**Can I cancel anytime?**
Yes. Cancel in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. You keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for.

**What happens to my alarms if I cancel?**
Your alarms stay on your iPhone.

## Data & Privacy

**Where are my alarms stored?**
On your iPhone. If you're on Plus and sign in, they're also backed up to the cloud so they follow you to a new iPhone.

**Do you sell my data?**
No. See the Privacy Policy in Settings for details.

**How do I delete my account?**
Settings → Account & Sync → Delete Account.

---

## Troubleshooting

**My alarm didn't ring.**
Open iOS Settings → Chronir and check that Alarms are allowed. Then open the alarm in Chronir and confirm it's turned on and shows the date you expect.

**I'm not getting pre-alarm warnings.**
Warnings are notifications, which iOS controls separately from alarms. In iOS Settings → Chronir → Notifications, turn on Allow Notifications. If you use a Focus, allow Chronir in it.
